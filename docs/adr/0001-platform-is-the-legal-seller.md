# The platform is the legal seller; Butchers are fulfilment partners

Customers buy every Order from the platform at one Platform Price, and dispatch offers it to Butchers in the Delivery Radius, the first to Accept fulfilling it for a privately agreed Fulfilment Fee. We first chose a marketplace with each Butcher as the legal seller, but making independent sellers charge one price is resale price maintenance under § 1 GWB / Art. 101 TFEU (Vertical Guidelines §§ 67(c), 194), while a platform that sells and picks who fulfils is expressly not (§ 193). See [the pricing research](https://github.com/denniskasper/ww-app/blob/research/platform-price-legality/research/platform-price-legality.md).

## Consequences

- The platform carries the seller's duties: allergen and product information before purchase, VAT on the full price, and liability toward the Customer.
- Payments are charged on the platform's own account, so no payment-services licence (ZAG) question arises and the Butcher need not be known at checkout.
- The Customer must not choose the Butcher while the price is fixed (§ 193), so Pickup is out of the MVP.
- No Butcher co-owns or governs the platform, and no Butcher sees another's terms, to avoid hub-and-spoke coordination.
