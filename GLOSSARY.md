# ww-app

Ordering Weißwurst breakfasts in München: a Customer orders, the nearest Butcher prepares, a Driver delivers.

## Language

### People and partners

**Customer**:
A person who places Orders through the app.
_Avoid_: User, buyer, client

**Butcher**:
A partner Metzgerei that fulfils Orders for the platform in return for a Fulfilment Fee. The Butcher is not the seller; the Customer buys from the platform.
_Avoid_: Vendor, merchant, restaurant, shop

**Driver**:
A person who collects an Order from the Butcher and brings it to the Customer.
_Avoid_: Courier, rider, delivery guy

**Courier Service**:
An outside company whose Drivers carry out Deliveries for the platform.
_Avoid_: Logistics partner, delivery provider

### Ordering

**Order**:
One Customer's request for Products, fulfilled by exactly one Butcher.
_Avoid_: Purchase, booking

**Product**:
An item a Customer can put in an Order, such as Weißwurst, Brezn, or süßer Senf.
_Avoid_: Item, article, SKU

**Platform Price**:
The single retail price at which the platform sells a Product to Customers.
_Avoid_: List price, standard price

**Fulfilment Fee**:
What the platform pays a Butcher for fulfilling one Order, agreed separately with each Butcher.
_Avoid_: Commission, payout, wholesale price

**ASAP Order**:
An Order to be fulfilled as soon as possible on the same morning.
_Avoid_: Instant order, now order

**Scheduled Order**:
An Order for a chosen morning time slot, placed in advance.
_Avoid_: Pre-order, booking

**Weißwurstfrühstück**:
A Product that bundles Weißwurst with Brezn and süßer Senf.
_Avoid_: Combo, menu, set

### Fulfillment

**Delivery**:
Fulfillment where a Driver brings the Order to the Customer's address.

**Pickup**:
Fulfillment where the Customer collects the Order at the Butcher.
_Avoid_: Collection, takeaway

**Delivery Radius**:
The area around a Customer's address within which Butchers are offered that Customer's Order.
_Avoid_: Zone, range

**Accept**:
A Butcher's explicit commitment to fulfil an offered Order. The first Butcher to Accept gets the Order.
_Avoid_: Claim, confirm

**Paused**:
The state of a Butcher who is temporarily not offered any Orders.
_Avoid_: Closed, offline, inactive
